Who we are

An Islamabad-based consultancy leading integrated planning, engineering and project management for large urban developments.

About the firm

Sani Development Consultants (SDC) focuses on large, integrated land-development schemes: residential communities, retail and commercial centres, and the infrastructure and public realm that support them.

SDC acts as lead consultant. It assembles developers, construction contractors, architects, engineering firms, town planners, quantity surveyors and environmental consultants from its Associate Network, then manages, coordinates and supervises them under a single Project Director — from first survey to final handover.
