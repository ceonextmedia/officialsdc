About the firm

Integrated delivery under one accountable lead

SDC focuses on large, integrated land-development schemes and manages a vetted Associate Network under a single Project Director.

Developers, construction contractors, architects, engineering firms, town planners, quantity surveyors and environmental consultants are assembled package by package — then coordinated and supervised from first survey to final handover.
