Services

What we do

A complete consultancy service, from master plan to handover, delivered by SDC and its associate firms under one management. Services are offered individually or as one integrated commission.

01

Master Planning

Site analysis, land use, plot and block layouts, density studies, zoning and phasing.

02

Urban Design

Public realm, streetscapes, parks, pedestrian networks and landscape concepts.

03

Architecture

Concept to detailed design of commercial blocks, housing and community buildings.

04

Civil & Structural

Structural design, geotechnical coordination, earthworks, grading and retaining structures.

05

MEP Engineering

Power distribution, street lighting, HVAC, plumbing, fire protection and life safety.

06

Infrastructure, Roads & Utilities

Road design, traffic and access, water supply, sewerage, drainage, gas and power networks.

07

Cost Consultancy & QS

Estimates, BOQs, rate analysis, cash-flow forecasts, payment certification and final accounts.

08

Project Management & Supervision

Programme control, resident supervision, quality testing, reporting, variations and claims.

09

Approvals & NOC Liaison

Submissions to LDA, EPA Punjab, WASA, LESCO, SNGPL and other agencies as applicable.

10

Developer Tendering & Contracts

Prequalification, tender documents, bid evaluation, award support and contract administration.

Key capabilities

What sets our delivery apart

Planning-led

Every package starts from the Client’s master plan and requirements, so design, cost and construction stay aligned with the approved vision.

Specialist-matched

Associate firms are selected package by package against documented criteria, so each part of the scheme is delivered by the right specialist.

Cost & schedule control

Baseline programme and cost plan, monthly variance reporting and certified payments keep the scheme on budget and on time.

Compliance by design

Approvals, environmental conditions and building regulations are built into each design stage rather than addressed at the end.
