Delivery model

How we deliver

A package-based delivery model, a transparent partner selection process and a seven-phase methodology with Client approval at every gate.

Associate network & delivery model

Each development package is awarded to a specialist associate developer or contractor. SDC carries out overall planning coordination, quality control, and cost and schedule management, with all work executed strictly to the Client’s master plan and requirements.

How the model works

  1. Client briefClient issues master plan, MoUs, project details and Terms of Reference.
  2. PackagingSDC organises the scheme into packages and design inputs.
  3. ShortlistSDC shortlists the best-suited associate firm per package.
  4. Client consentProposed firms and key staff are submitted for Client consent.
  5. MobilisationMoUs are signed; firms are mobilised to the agreed programme.
  6. SupervisionSDC manages, coordinates and supervises through handover.

Partner selection

The Associate Network brings together established developers, construction contractors, architects, engineering firms, town planners, quantity surveyors and environmental consultants. For each package, firms are pre-qualified and selected through a documented process whose results are shared with the Client.

  • PEC registration & category — valid registration in the required discipline
  • Similar past projects — comparable type, scale and complexity
  • Financial capacity — turnover, liquidity and ability to resource the package
  • HSE record — safety management system, incident history and site practice
  • Team strength — qualified, registered key staff, equipment and resources

Selection steps

Long-list→Pre-qualify→Evaluate→Recommend→Agree→Review

Roles

Client

Issues the brief and ToR; approves deliverables, associate firms and key personnel; decides at each phase gate.

SDC

Planning coordination, quality control, cost and schedule management, approvals, tendering, supervision and reporting, strictly to the master plan.

Associates

Developers and contractors execute packages; associate consultants provide design and specialist input, all to SDC’s standards.

Approach & methodology

Seven phases, each closed by Client approval

Each phase ends with a deliverable submitted for the Client’s formal approval before the next begins.

  1. Survey & InvestigationsTopographic and boundary survey, geotechnical investigation, utilities and drainage survey, traffic counts, environmental baseline.Deliverable: Survey and site constraints report
  2. Master PlanningAgreed brief; land-use options; area statements; infrastructure concept; phasing.Deliverable: Preferred master plan, preliminary estimate
  3. DesignCoordinated preliminary and detailed design across architecture, structure, MEP, roads, water, sewerage, drainage, power and gas.Deliverable: Drawings, specifications, BOQs, engineer’s estimate
  4. Approvals & NOCsSubmissions and liaison with the competent authority, EPA Punjab (IEE/EIA), WASA, LESCO, SNGPL and others as applicable.Deliverable: Approved plans, NOCs, environmental approval
  5. TenderingPackaging strategy, developer/contractor prequalification, tender documents, bid evaluation and award recommendation.Deliverable: Tender documents, evaluation report
  6. Construction SupervisionResident engineering, material testing, quality inspections, HSE oversight, measurement and payment certification.Deliverable: Monthly reports, IPCs, QA/HSE records
  7. Commissioning & HandoverTesting and commissioning, snagging, as-built drawings, O&M manuals, defects monitoring and final account.Deliverable: As-builts, O&M manuals, handover

Management controls throughout

  • Documented reporting line to the Client’s representative
  • Baseline programme and cost plan; monthly variance reports
  • Stage design reviews and cross-package coordination
  • Risk register covering land, approvals, utilities, cost and security

Quality, HSE & compliance

Commitments we hold ourselves and our associates to

Quality

Project Quality Plan binding on every associate firm; design checking, document control, inspection and test plans; non-conformance tracking to closure; quality management aligned with ISO 9001 principles.

Health, Safety & Environment

Contractor site HSE plans, monitored and enforced; toolbox talks, incident reporting, PPE discipline; compliance with applicable environmental protection law; dust, noise, waste, water and tree protection.

Compliance & Ethics

PEC, PCATP, competent-authority and fire-safety rules; open, documented tendering; conflict-of-interest safeguards; confidentiality and security vetting where required; anti-corruption commitment; Integrity Pact if required.

Registration and statutory documents, CVs, associate firm MoUs and project references are available on request.
